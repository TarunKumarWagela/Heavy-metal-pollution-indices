Scientific Methodology Reference
Heavy Metal Pollution Index (HPI)
Wi = 1 / Si Qi = |Mi - Ii| / |Si - Ii| × 100 HPI = Σ(Wi×Qi) / Σ(Wi) Effective contrib = (Wi×Qi) / Σ(Wi) % contrib = (Wi×Qi) / Σ(Wi×Qi) × 100
Mohan et al. (1996). J. Environ. Sci. Health, 31(2), 283–289
Heavy Metal Evaluation Index (HEI)
HEI = Σ (Hc_i / Hmac_i) Hc_i = measured concentration Hmac_i = max acceptable concentration
Edet & Offiong (2002). GeoJournal, 57(4), 295–304
Degree of Contamination (Cd Index)
CF_i = (C_A_i / C_N_i) - 1 Cd_index = Σ CF_i ⚠ Cd here = INDEX name, NOT cadmium element Signed (Backman 1998): preserves negative values
Backman et al. (1998). Environ. Geology, 36(1–2), 55–64
