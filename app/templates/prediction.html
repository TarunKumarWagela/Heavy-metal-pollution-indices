Prediction Limitations
  • Models are trained exclusively on uploaded real measurement data.
  • Spatial generalization is limited to the training data region.
  • BDL (below detection limit) samples are excluded from training.
  • Do not use predictions as a sole basis for regulatory decisions.
  • The target metal is never included as an input feature (no data leakage).
  • Confidence intervals are based on Random Forest tree variance (±1.96σ).
